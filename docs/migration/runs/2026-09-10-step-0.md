2. `app/admin/migration/page.tsx` — `faq_question_missing_in_export` → `faq_missing_in_source` and

`source_status`, `review_flags`, `legacy_pricing_copy` and `legacy_thumbnail_id` columns on
`site.cities`. Never hand-written. Without it the build fails at page collection with
`column "source_status" of relation "cities" does not exist`.
