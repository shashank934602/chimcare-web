## 2. Phase 2 — the CityPage overflow

The brief carried Step 1's finding forward as an open defect. It was fixed during Step 1, and this
run re-verified it rather than taking the automated result on trust.

A new check, `scripts/test/viewport-real.mjs`, opens a browser window at the requested size instead
of emulating one, and cross-checks four numbers that must agree — layout viewport, document scroll
width, body scroll width, visual viewport — then **tries to scroll the page sideways** and reports
how far it moved. That last one is what a visitor would actually notice.

It also refuses to report a width it did not reach: Chrome will not open a window narrower than about
500px, so 390px is device-emulated and the output says so on every line rather than silently
measuring 500 and calling it 390.

Result: 15/15 clean on the fixture pages, 18/18 clean on the real rendered pages, at all three widths,
with zero sideways scroll everywhere. The Step 1 fixes hold: `min-width: 0` on the sticky bar's flex
items, and dialogs rendered as siblings of `<main>`.

No new overflow was found, and none was invented to have something to fix.

## 3. Phase 3 — connecting real source data

The app already had the pipeline. What it did not have was two of the three source datasets wired
into it, so **every one of the 134 Minnesota city pages answered 404**: `data/seed/minnesota.ts`
hard-coded `faqs = []` and `heroImageKey = null`, and the gate failed on all of them.

`minnesota.faq.json` (642 real FAQ pairs) and `minnesota.media.json` (15 byte-verified heroes) were
regenerated in Step 0 and were sitting unused. Both are now read, keyed by the city's own WordPress
slug. A city missing from either keeps a null field and a flag; nothing is borrowed from another city,
and an asset that failed byte verification is not used at all.

After wiring: 110 publishable, 24 needs_review, 642 FAQ pairs across 134 cities, 134 heroes, 133 with
WordPress alt text. The one without alt (Wayzata) keeps a null and a flag.

## 6. Failures found, and what was done about each

The first parity run was **SMOKE FAIL** — 4 critical failures. Each was taken to a root cause before
anything was changed.

**S09 and S10 — production 200, local 404, on the two highest-traffic city pages in Minnesota.**
`build-mn-seed.mjs` skipped branch pages entirely when reading their "why it matters" prose, and
searched for the heading wording only coverage pages use. Branch pages write
`Why {City}, MN Homeowners Trust Chimcare` and put the paragraph in a styled `<span>`, not a `<p>`.
All 14 branch cities failed the gate on prose that was in WordPress the whole time. Fixed by matching
both heading forms and taking the block between headings. ISSUE-027, DECISION-016.

**S07 and S08 — legacy URLs 404 locally.** By design: the dispatcher still 404s `kind='legacy'`.
Rendered through a new admin-gated preview at `/admin/preview/legacy/{pilot}/`; production routing
untouched and the 404 recorded as an expected state. DECISION-017.

**S07 and S08 — missing canonical, and noindex.** Properties of the preview route, not the template.
The checks now record them as untestable for legacy until LegacyPage is routed, rather than passing
them or making the preview indexable to suit the test. DECISION-018.

Re-run after the fixes: **0 critical, 10 REVIEW.**
